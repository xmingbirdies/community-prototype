Prototype

Community · Phase 1

Everything here is clickable. The data is made up and resets when you tap Reset.

  • Friends and Interests are the two entry points at the top of Community.
  • Try the swipe Discover: drag a card right to follow, left to skip, or use the buttons. Tap a card to watch its videos full screen, then swipe up for more.
  • Following a friend: unfollow keeps the friendship. Contributors unfollow with an Undo.
  • Profile ••• has Mute, Not interested, Report and Block.
  • Events moved to the Play tab.
  • Topics (learn topics and local topics) are a later-phase exploration, not Phase 1 scope.

Colors, type sizes, icons, toolbars and the tab bar follow the Figma library Design System (2026). Text uses ABC Monument Grotesk and numbers use EK Modena, as in the design system.

On a phone, open the hosted version and use Add to Home Screen to install it.
